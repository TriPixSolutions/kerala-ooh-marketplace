# Your Ad Space — Brand Foundation

## Brand name

**Your Ad Space**

Use title case in normal product copy. Use `YOUR AD SPACE` only where an all-caps lockup is intentional.

## Product positioning

Your Ad Space is a premium discovery and enquiry platform for outdoor advertising inventory across Kerala. It connects businesses and agencies with verified advertising spaces such as billboards, hoardings, LED screens, restaurant displays, mall screens, and other public-space media.

## Brand promise

Make every advertising space easier to discover, understand, compare, and enquire about.

## Primary audience

- Businesses looking for physical advertising placements
- Marketing and advertising agencies
- Owners and operators of advertising spaces

## Voice

Clear, confident, useful, modern, and human.

Avoid exaggerated startup language, unnecessary jargon, and generic AI-style copy.

## Visual direction

The visual quality should feel premium and contemporary, inspired by the discipline of high-end technology products without copying another brand's layout.

Principles:

- Clean hierarchy
- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Real location photography
- Restrained use of color
- High-quality listing cards
- Subtle motion
- Clear calls to action
- Mobile-first interaction design

## Initial color foundation

The system should begin with a neutral foundation:

- Ink: #111318
- Slate: #5F6570
- Surface: #F7F8FA
- White: #FFFFFF
- Border: #E5E7EB
- Accent: reserved for one strong brand/action color after visual validation

Do not introduce a large rainbow palette. Color should communicate state, hierarchy, and action.

## Typography direction

Primary UI font: **Inter** or a comparable modern grotesk with excellent screen readability.

Use weight and spacing to create hierarchy rather than excessive font-size variation.

## UI character

Rounded but not playful. Premium but not luxury-fashion. Modern but not futuristic for the sake of appearance. The interface should feel like mature software that has been refined over years.

## Naming

Repository: `kerala-ooh-marketplace`
Product: `Your Ad Space`
Internal shorthand: `YAS`

## Core experience sentence

**Find the space. Understand the opportunity. Start the conversation.**

## Important product rule

The brand is not a generic advertising agency website. It is a marketplace and discovery platform. Every design decision should reinforce discovery, comparison, trust, location context, availability, and enquiry.
